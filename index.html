About Athryn

Built to Create. Ready to Produce.

Athryn Creative Industries is an apparel and textile production company based in Kabupaten Bandung, West Java. Established in 2019, we provide production solutions for companies, organizations, brands, communities, and various other needs.

From everyday apparel to uniforms and custom products, we work with customers to turn ideas and requirements into finished products through a flexible production process.

  • Since 2019

    Experience built through years of apparel production.

  • Custom Production

    Products can be adapted to specific designs, materials, and requirements.

  • Flexible Solutions

    Production approaches are adjusted to each project's needs.

  • Based in Bandung

    Serving customers from Bandung and beyond.

Our Process

  1. Step 01

    Discuss

    Understand customer needs, product type, quantity, specifications, and requirements.

  2. Step 02

    Design & Specification

    Finalize design, measurements, materials, colors, and production specifications.

  3. Step 03

    Material Preparation

    Prepare the required fabrics and supporting materials.

  4. Step 04

    Cutting & Sewing

    Production begins based on the agreed specifications.

  5. Step 05

    Finishing & QC

    Products are checked, cleaned, finished, and prepared for delivery.

  6. Step 06

    Delivery
